Mobile Coverage Analysis

ArcGIS Online · Dashboards · Spatial Analysis

Overview

Mapped mobile coverage, road connectivity and dwellings outside mapped coverage to support regional planning and stakeholder reporting.

My contribution

  • Integrated coverage, mobile tower, road and dwelling data.
  • Analysed dwelling locations against mapped coverage.
  • Built an interactive dashboard showing coverage gaps and road connectivity.

Workflow

Coverage & dwelling data → Spatial analysis → Interactive dashboard

Result

A shared view of mapped connectivity gaps for Council planning and stakeholder discussions.

Dwelling coverage results reflect the source coverage data; they do not confirm reception at each property.
