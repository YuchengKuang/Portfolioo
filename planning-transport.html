Internal Council web maps

ArcGIS Pro · ArcGIS Online

Overview

Centralised spatial data from NSW Planning Portal, SEED, heritage, utility providers, TfNSW and Council into dedicated web maps for Planning and Roads & Transport teams.

My contribution

  • Sourced, cleaned and structured datasets from multiple organisations.
  • Organised layers and symbology around departmental needs.
  • Configured map tools and interfaces for exploring planning constraints and infrastructure.
  • Created internal metadata documenting data sources and supporting future updates.

Workflow

External & Council data → Clean & structure → ArcGIS Online → Departmental web maps

Result

One place for staff to explore planning constraints, infrastructure and asset context during Council enquiries.
