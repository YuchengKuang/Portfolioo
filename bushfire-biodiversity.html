Bushfire & Biodiversity Research

Master’s dissertation · RMIT University

ArcGIS Pro · Python · GeoPandas · Spatial analysis · Remote sensing

Overview

Dissertation: Biodiversity Risk Associated with Australian Bushfires

Analysed bushfire patterns and biodiversity exposure using historical fire records, bushfire-prone land, vegetation and environmental datasets.

My contribution

  • Integrated spatial datasets to examine fire patterns and biodiversity exposure.
  • Used Python to prepare fire records, check dates and remove records with missing ignition dates.
  • Summarised annual recorded burned area and fire counts.
  • Applied spatial analysis and remote sensing methods to map environmental risk patterns.

Workflow

Fire & environmental data → Python preparation → Spatial & temporal analysis → Maps & charts

Result

Produced maps and analytical outputs to communicate bushfire patterns and biodiversity exposure, informed by fire management and prescribed burning context.
