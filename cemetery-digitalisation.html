Cemetery Digitalisation

Designed and built from the ground up

ArcGIS Pro · ArcGIS Online · Excel

Overview

Built a cemetery GIS from the ground up, migrating spreadsheets into mapped plots linked to consistent burial records.

My contribution

  • Designed feature classes, lookup values and the record structure.
  • Developed the migration approach from spreadsheets to GIS.
  • Aligned mapped plots with section and plot references in source records.
  • Linked plot geometry to burial records and configured record lookup and editing tools for the cemetery manager.

Workflow

Spreadsheets → Data model & migration → Plot geometry & record relationships → Cemetery map

Result

Improved access to cemetery records for customer service and long-term management, reducing reliance on manual spreadsheets.
